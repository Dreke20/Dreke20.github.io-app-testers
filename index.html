These Android apps are in Google Play closed testing. The steps are the same for every app — pick one below and follow them in order.

How to become a tester

Use the same Google account that is signed in on your phone’s Play Store for every step.

1

Join that app’s Google Group

Join with the same Google account as your Play Store. If a group is still awaiting Google approval, that card will say “Opening soon” and the links will stay locked.

2

Open the opt-in link

Tap Become a tester on the official Google Play opt-in page for that app.

3

Wait, then install

Wait a few minutes, then install the app from its Play Store link.

4

Stay for 14 days

Stay opted in, keep the app installed for 14 days, and open it a few times.

Test-for-test

If you found this from a Reddit or Discord tester thread, post your own opt-in link in that same thread after you join, opt in, and install. Dreke will test your app back.

There’s no form and no email signup here, and this page doesn’t collect any data. Just leave your link on the thread where you found this, and keep both apps installed for 14 days.

FAQ

Why 14 days?

Google Play requires testers to stay opted in for 14 consecutive days before a new personal developer account can apply for production. Uninstalling early can reset that clock.

It says “App not found.” What now?

That usually means you’re signed into a different Google account than the one that joined the tester group, or you haven’t opted in yet. Confirm the accounts match, finish the opt-in step, then wait a few minutes and try the Play Store link again.

What phone do I need?

Android only, through Google Play. These tests aren’t available on iPhone.

Is it free?

Yes. Joining a closed test and installing the app is free.
